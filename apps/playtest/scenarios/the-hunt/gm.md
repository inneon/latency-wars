## Starting state (end of turn 0)

The game starts a couple of turns into the opening: both flagships have moved forward, Line fleets are heading for the centre, and each Raider is one hex from its side's magnetosphere pocket. Treat every unit as having held its starting position (listed under "Starting units" below) since long before turn 1. No unit has any enemy within sensor range, so neither player has any contacts. Doctrines are Balanced, with each fleet's home at its starting hex. Neither player has any probes yet; the first arrive in turn 1's Economy phase.

Links: Blue Node West ↔ Blue Node North (length 8). Red Node East ↔ Red Node South (length 8). Each player produces 64 ÷ 16 = 4 qBits per turn and starts with 8 qBits banked.

No unit has an active order. Every fleet uses doctrine defaults from the mechanics doc.

## Notes for the GM

- The scenario is symmetric under (q, r) → (−q, −r). Neither side has an advantage.
- Each node is far from its own flagship and relatively close to the enemy's assets (for example Blue Node North is 5 from Red Node East and 6 from the Red Flagship). That's deliberate: the long, valuable link is exposed.
- The Trojan fields give a web room to grow, or to recover after losing a node somewhere other than the zone it lost.
  - With pocket A built, a Blue node in the SW field adds about 7.8 qBits a turn, and one in the NE field about 6.1. Red's figures are the mirror image.
  - Each side's better field sits 2 hexes from the enemy's pocket, so it is exposed.
  - With pocket A and the SW field built, losing any single Blue node still leaves at least about 7 qBits a turn, not 0.5. With the NE field instead, losing Node West still leaves only about 1.6.
