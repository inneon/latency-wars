# Hints for Ycnetal

- **Turn 1:** your Line, Raider and Probe are on your home planet, 2 hexes from your flagship, so a light order sent on turn 1 reaches them on turn 2. An instant order gets them moving on turn 1 for 4 qBits each.
- **Speeds:** a Raider (speed 2) reaches a site 9 hexes away, such as Langis, in 5 turns; a Line (speed 1) takes 9. A probe also moves at 2.
- **What a node adds to a web** (Blue's figures; Red's are the mirror image). The starting web makes 78 ÷ 16 ≈ 4.9 qBits a turn, and almost all of it comes from the Langis node.

| Blue's nodes | Σ length² | qBits a turn |
|---|---|---|
| Start: Thgil L1, L2 and Langis L4 | 78 | 4.9 |
| + Thgil L5 / Yaled L3 (0, 6) | 172 | 10.8 |
| + Thgil L4 / Yaled L5 (6, −6) | 244 | 15.3 |
| + Langis L2 (−4, 4) | 296 | 18.5 |
| + both (0, 6) and (6, −6) | 482 | 30.1 |

- **Losing the Langis node** takes the starting web from 4.9 to 0.25 a turn.
